For every student who has ever re-sat an exam that someone else leaked

We make a leaked paper worthless.

NEOI is a proposed national institution that runs the security of India's public examinations, so that even if a question is stolen, it cannot be sold.

हर उस विद्यार्थी के लिए जिसे किसी और के लीक की वजह से दोबारा परीक्षा देनी पड़ी

हम लीक हुए पेपर को बेकार बना देते हैं।

NEOI एक प्रस्तावित राष्ट्रीय संस्थान है जो भारत की सार्वजनिक परीक्षाओं की सुरक्षा संभालता है, ताकि कोई प्रश्न चोरी हो भी जाए, तो वह बिक न सके।

Exam owners like CBSE, NTA, UPSC and the IIMs keep every academic decision. NEOI secures the journey from the question pool to the published result.

Leaks are a market. Punishment alone has not shut it down.

Penalties have risen, most recently under the 2026 Amendment to the Public Examinations Act. The price of a leaked paper has not fallen. NEOI is designed to change that.

        Inside NEOI: 860 people across three levels

        Standards, keys and evidence sit at the national core. Printing, custody and centres are run from five regional hubs. On exam day, three NEOI people join every centre. Choose a team to see what it does and which stages it touches.
